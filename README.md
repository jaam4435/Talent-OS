# TalentOS

**WhatsApp-first workforce operating system** for creative agencies, SaaS companies, AI startups, content studios, and remote teams.

TalentOS manages the complete lifecycle of creative talent — from discovery and opportunity broadcasting through project execution, deliverable approval, and payment.

## Core Capabilities

1. Talent Discovery & Database
2. Opportunity Broadcasting (WhatsApp)
3. Response Collection & Shortlisting
4. Sample & Project Assignment
5. Task Tracking & Communication
6. Deliverable Submission & Revision Management
7. Approval & Payment Workflows
8. Performance Analytics & AI Matching
9. Resource Planning & Capacity Management
